Good to know before you start

Invoice prepares documents from the information you enter. It does not send them to a tax authority and is not a certified e-invoicing system, so check whether your country requires one.

Where the app can’t issue invoices

Invoice cannot issue invoices or credit notes for sellers based in Spain, Italy, Portugal, Romania, Hungary, Mexico, Brazil, Argentina, Chile, Colombia, Peru, Ecuador, Uruguay or Costa Rica. The same applies to business-to-business invoices from sellers in Poland, Croatia or Greece, and to invoices from VAT-registered sellers in Belgium to Belgian business clients. In these cases an official or certified invoicing system is required.

You can still create quotes and pro formas there and issue the invoice itself in an official tool. Deposits, payments and client balances only work with invoices issued in the app.

The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob and partner networks, which collect device identifiers, approximate location, usage and diagnostic data to show and measure ads (the app asks for consent where required). They never receive your quotes, invoices or client details. Details in the Privacy Policy.
